Why we started

We kept downloading apps that uploaded documents to cloud servers we'd never agreed to. Cycle trackers sharing health data with advertisers. Sleep apps that needed internet access to play a rain sound.

As engineers, we knew this wasn't technically necessary — it was a choice. We made a different one.

Privacy isn't a premium feature. Working offline shouldn't require a subscription.

How we build

Three principles we've held since day one — proven by the apps we ship.

Offline-First

Every app works completely without internet. No cloud dependency, no service outages, no waiting for a server response.

See it in AbhiScan →

Privacy-First

Your data never leaves your device unless you share it. We don't operate user data servers. We can't see your data because we never receive it.

See it in Lunavi →

Quality-Obsessed

No ads. No dark patterns. No feature bloat. Every app ships when it's genuinely good — not when a roadmap says so.
