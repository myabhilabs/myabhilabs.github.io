Privacy Policy

AI QR Barcode Scanner · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

AI QR Barcode Scanner is built on a local-first architecture. All data you scan, create, or mark as a favourite is stored strictly locally on your device's internal database. No external servers maintain your scan history or images.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No account is required. The following clarifies what stays on your device:

  • Scan history, favourites, and created QR codes are stored in a local on-device database
  • QR code safety verification uses Google ML Kit entirely on-device — no data is sent to external servers
  • No data is transmitted to any server we operate

Camera Permission

AI QR Barcode Scanner requests camera access solely to scan QR codes and barcodes. All image processing happens on-device. Camera frames are never transmitted to any server.

Location Permission

Android requires location permission to facilitate connecting to WiFi networks when you scan a WiFi QR code. This permission is used solely for that purpose — we do not track your location, store it, or transmit it.

Other Permissions

  • WiFi State: Required to read network information when connecting via a scanned WiFi QR code
  • Vibration: Used to provide haptic feedback on successful scans
  • Internet: Required for displaying ads via Google AdMob

On-Device Safety Verification

Scanned URLs and codes are verified for safety using Google ML Kit, which processes and verifies codes entirely on your device. No scanned content is sent to external servers for verification.

Advertising

AI QR Barcode Scanner displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to measure ad performance and serve relevant ads.

  • EEA & UK users: A consent form is presented at first launch via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Personalized ads require your explicit consent.
  • California residents: You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your Rights

EEA & UK (GDPR): You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data; to withdraw advertising consent at any time; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what data is collected about you, to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data), and to request deletion of data held by our advertising partners.

Data Deletion

Since AI QR Barcode Scanner stores everything locally, you are in full control:

  • Delete individual scan history entries or favourites directly within the app
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → AI QR Barcode → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all app data permanently

There is no remote account to delete. For advertising data held by Google, reset your Android Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email us at contact@abhilabs.app.
