Privacy Policy

AI QR Barcode · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

AI QR Barcode does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All scanning happens locally on your device.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No account is required
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • No advertising networks
  • No data transmitted to any server

Camera Permission

AI QR Barcode requests camera access solely to scan QR codes and barcodes. Camera images are processed locally and never transmitted.

Data Retention

Scan results remain on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.
