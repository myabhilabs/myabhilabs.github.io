Privacy Policy

AbhiScan Pro · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

AbhiScan Pro does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. All scanning, OCR, and PDF generation happens locally on your device.

Data We Collect

We collect no personal data. Specifically:

  • No account is required
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • No advertising networks
  • No data transmitted to any server

Camera Permission

AbhiScan Pro requests camera access solely to scan documents. Camera images are processed locally and never transmitted.

Storage Permission

PDF files are saved to your device's local storage at your direction. We do not access any other files on your device.

Data Retention

Your scanned documents remain on your device until you delete them. Uninstalling the app removes all app data.

Contact

Questions? Email us at myabhilabs@gmail.com.
