01

Summary

AbhiScan is built on a local-first architecture. Your scanned documents, PDFs, and extracted text never leave your device unless you explicitly share them. We do not own, operate, or rent servers to store your documents. No advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK is bundled in the app.

We cannot access your documents — no server exists that could receive them.

02

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No account is required. The following clarifies exactly what stays on your device:

  • Scanned images are processed locally using OpenCV edge detection
  • OCR text extraction is stored in a local SQLite full-text search database on your device
  • Generated PDF files are saved via the Android Storage Access Framework at your direction
  • No data is transmitted to any server we operate
  • No advertising or analytics SDK is present — the app never reads your Android Advertising ID
03

Permissions

AbhiScan requests two Android permissions. Here is exactly what each is used for — and what it cannot do.

Permission Purpose Cannot do
Camera Scan documents. All image processing (edge detection, perspective correction, contrast) happens on-device. Transmit images to any server
Storage Save PDFs and scanned images to your device via the Android Storage Access Framework at your direction. Access files outside what you explicitly open
04

Diagnostics

AbhiScan embeds no crash-reporting or analytics SDK. Google Play collects anonymized crash and performance data (Android Vitals) for every app distributed through the Play Store. That collection is performed by Google at the platform level, not by code we ship, and the reports contain device model, OS version, and stack traces — never your documents, scanned text, or personal data.

This data is only shared by devices where the owner opted in to Android's usage and diagnostics sharing; you can change that in Android Settings → Google → Usage & diagnostics (on some devices, Settings → Privacy → Usage & diagnostics). Google's handling of that data is described in its privacy policy.

05

Your Rights

AbhiScan processes no personal data, so most data-subject requests have nothing to act on — there is no profile, account, or identifier tied to you anywhere in our systems.

EEA & UK (GDPR): No personal data is collected, so no consent form is shown and no legal basis for processing is required. You retain the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): We do not collect, sell, or share personal information, and no advertising partner holds data obtained through this app. Requests to know, delete, or opt out have no data to apply to.

06

Data Deletion

Since AbhiScan stores everything locally, you are in full control:

  • Delete individual scans or PDFs directly within the app
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → AbhiScan → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all app data permanently

There is no remote account to delete, and no advertising or analytics partner holds data from this app.

Local deletion is immediate and permanent. We hold no copy to recover from.

07

Contact
