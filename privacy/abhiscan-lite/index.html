Privacy Policy

AbhiScan Lite · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

AbhiScan Lite is built on a local-first architecture. Your scanned images, generated PDFs, and extracted text never leave your device unless you explicitly share them. We do not own, operate, or rent servers to store your documents.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No account is required to use AbhiScan Lite. The following clarifies exactly what stays on your device:

  • Scanned images are processed locally using on-device edge detection
  • OCR text extraction is stored in a local SQLite database on your device
  • Generated PDF files are saved via the Android Storage Access Framework at your direction
  • No data is transmitted to any server we operate

Camera Permission

AbhiScan Lite requests camera access solely to scan documents. All image processing happens on-device. Camera images are never transmitted to any server.

Storage Permission

PDF files and scanned images are saved to your device's local storage via the Android Storage Access Framework. We access only the files you explicitly open or save through the app.

Advertising

AbhiScan Lite displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to measure ad performance and serve relevant ads.

  • EEA & UK users: A consent form is presented at first launch via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Personalized ads require your explicit consent.
  • California residents: You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • You can review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Crash Reporting

Anonymized crash reports are collected via Google Play Vitals and Firebase Crashlytics to help us fix bugs. These reports contain device model, OS version, and stack traces — never your documents, scanned text, or personal data.

Your Rights

EEA & UK (GDPR): You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data; to withdraw advertising consent at any time; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what data is collected about you, to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data), and to request deletion of data held by our advertising partners.

Data Deletion

Since AbhiScan Lite stores everything locally, you are in full control:

  • Delete individual scans or PDFs directly within the app
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → AbhiScan Lite → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all app data permanently

There is no remote account to delete. For advertising data held by Google, reset your Android Advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email us at contact@abhilabs.app.
