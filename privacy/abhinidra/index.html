01

Summary

Your sleep is private. AbhiNidra is designed with an offline-first architecture — your data stays on your device and is never sold. We do not own, operate, or rent servers for user data storage.

We cannot access your sleep data or audio — no server exists that could receive them.

02

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. Specifically, AbhiNidra does not gather:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics
  • Health or sleep quality information
  • Audio recordings of any kind

No account is required. The service operates without servers for user data storage.

03

Microphone Permission (Adaptive Volume)

The optional Adaptive Volume feature uses your microphone to detect ambient noise levels and automatically adjust playback volume. Audio is processed locally in real time — the decibel level is calculated on-device and the audio buffer is discarded immediately after the calculation completes, within milliseconds.

No audio is ever recorded, stored, or transmitted. If you do not enable Adaptive Volume, microphone access is never requested.

Audio buffer is discarded within milliseconds of volume calculation. Nothing is stored or transmitted.

04

Local Storage

Custom sound presets, sleep journal entries, and sleep session history are stored exclusively on your device. This data is deleted permanently when you uninstall the app. The developer has no ability to access it.

05

Background Services

AbhiNidra runs two foreground services, both displaying persistent notifications as required by Android:

  • Media Playback Service: Keeps sleep sounds playing when the screen is off
  • Microphone Monitoring Service: Active only when Adaptive Volume is enabled; monitors ambient noise level to adjust volume

These services operate entirely on-device and do not transmit any data.

06

Advertising

AbhiNidra displays ads via Google AdMob — a third party that collects its own data. Ads appear in menus only — never during sleep sessions or audio playback.

AdMob may collect device identifiers (Android Advertising ID) and IP address to serve and measure ads.

  • EEA & UK users: A consent form is presented at first launch via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Personalized ads require your explicit consent.
  • California residents: You can opt out of personalized ads by resetting your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Review Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.
07

Your Rights

EEA & UK (GDPR): You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data; to withdraw advertising consent at any time; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what data is collected about you, to opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell data), and to request deletion of data held by our advertising partners.

08

Children

AbhiNidra is not directed at children under 13. The app includes baby and infant sleep sound content intended for use by parents and caregivers. The offline-first design means no data from any user — including children — is collected or transmitted.
