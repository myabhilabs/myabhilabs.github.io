01

Summary

AbhiNow ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by Abhi Labs. AbhiNow is private by default: it works on your device, without an account, and we do not collect personal data on our servers.

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • Your app-usage data, shields, settings, and history stay on your device.
  • No ads, no third-party trackers, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that send your activity off the device.
  • The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous purchase-validation data described in the Purchases section.

Because your shields, history, and settings never reach us, we have no ability to access, share, or recover them.

02

Information We Handle

Data stored only on your device

Your shields, schedules, settings, presence score, intercept history, and similar app data are stored locally on your device. They are not transmitted to us or anyone else, and are removed when you uninstall the app. Because this data never reaches us, we have no ability to access, share, or recover it.

If you contact us

If you email us (for example, via the app's "Send feedback" option), we receive your email address and the contents of your message, and use them only to respond to you.

Backups you export

Backup files you export from the app are created at your request, saved to a location you choose, and are not transmitted to us. Please store them safely.

03

Accessibility Service

With your explicit consent, AbhiNow uses Android's Accessibility Service to detect which app is currently in the foreground, so it can show a brief on-screen pause when you open an app you have chosen to shield. The service reads only the package name of the foreground app.

It does not read your screen content, what you type, your messages, or your passwords, and it does not transmit any such information off your device.

04

Usage Access (Optional)

If you grant Usage Access, AbhiNow reads your device's app-usage statistics to show screen-time insights and power usage-based shields. This is processed only on your device and is not transmitted to us.

05

Purchases & Premium Validation

To offer Premium, the app communicates with our payments provider, RevenueCat, Inc. This happens even if you never make a purchase: the app sends a randomly generated identifier (not your name or email), basic device information (device model, OS version, app version, locale), and your IP address, which RevenueCat uses to check Premium availability and validate entitlements.

If you buy Premium, your purchase is processed by Google Play Billing, and your purchase/entitlement record is handled by RevenueCat solely to validate your access to Premium. We never receive your card or financial account details; those are handled by Google.

No app-usage data, shield configuration, intercept history, or screen-time information is ever sent to RevenueCat, Google, or us.

06

Third Parties

  • Google Play Billing (Google LLC) — processes your purchase. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — validates your subscription/entitlement on our behalf as a service provider. RevenueCat processes data in the United States; transfers from the EEA/UK are protected by standard contractual clauses and/or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or for cross-context behavioral advertising. The app contains no advertising SDKs and does not use the Android Advertising ID.

Google Play may provide us with aggregated, anonymized crash and performance statistics through the Play Console; the app itself contains no crash-reporting or analytics SDK.

07

Data Retention & Deletion

Your app data stays on your device; you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling AbhiNow.

Purchase/entitlement records are retained for as long as needed to provide Premium and to meet legal obligations (for example, tax and accounting requirements). To request deletion of the purchase/entitlement data held by us via RevenueCat, email contact@abhilabs.app from the address linked to your Google Play purchase, with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete your associated records within 30 days. Google retains its own transaction records as required by its policies and applicable law, which we cannot delete.

08

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA, the UK, or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because AbhiNow stores your data on your device and we hold only the purchase-validation data described in the Purchases section (processed via RevenueCat and Google), you can exercise most of these rights by managing or deleting the app, or by emailing contact@abhilabs.app.

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

If you are in the EEA or UK: the legal basis for processing your purchase record is the performance of our contract with you (providing Premium) and our legitimate interest in validating entitlements and preventing fraud. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.

09

Children

AbhiNow is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10

Security

Data stored on your device is protected by Android's application sandbox. We operate no servers that store your data. The limited purchase-validation data described in the Purchases section is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Changes: We may update this Privacy Policy. Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
