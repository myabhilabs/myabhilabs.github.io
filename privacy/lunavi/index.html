01

Summary

Lunavi works entirely offline with zero cloud uploads. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send your logged periods, symptoms, moods or ovulation predictions anywhere. They leave your device only if you export or share them yourself. We do not own, operate, or rent servers for user data storage.

Lunavi is a personal wellness tracker for periods, ovulation and symptoms. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

The developer cannot access your health data — there is no server connection to receive it.

02

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No account is required. Lunavi contains no advertising SDKs and displays zero advertisements. Google Play handles some data separately (see Google Play), and we receive anything you choose to email us (see Contact). The following clarifies what stays on your device:

  • Cycle data, symptoms, moods, and predictions are stored in an encrypted database on your device
  • The developer cannot access this information — there is no server connection
  • No data is transmitted to any server we operate
03

Health & Cycle Data

All health and cycle data you enter — period dates and flow, symptoms and moods, basal body temperature, cervical mucus, ovulation test results, intercourse logs, weight, water, sleep details, exercise, medications, pill schedule and history, notes, and any other details you log or import — and the ovulation and period predictions Lunavi estimates from it are stored only on your device, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher. The encryption key is protected by the Android Keystore and stays on your device.

Your age group, goal, trying-to-conceive start date, optional PIN hint and app settings are kept on your device in encrypted storage. Your PIN and recovery key are stored only as salted hashes.

The app never sends this data to any server or third party. It leaves your device only if you export or share it yourself. Because no remote account exists, the developer has no ability to access your health data.

With no internet permission and no server, Lunavi gives no one a way to reach your data remotely — by design.

04

Device Permissions

  • Camera: used only for the OPK strip scanner, and Lunavi asks first. The photo is read in memory on your device and then discarded. It is not saved or sent anywhere. Only the result you choose to save is stored.
  • Notifications: used for reminders and cycle alerts, which are created on your device. Android may show them on your lock screen and on paired devices such as a watch. Discreet Mode keeps health details out of the text.
  • Biometrics: used for App Lock if you turn it on. Android checks your fingerprint or face and tells Lunavi only whether it matched.
  • Restart: used to reschedule your reminders after your phone restarts.

Android libraries used by the app also add wake lock, foreground service and network state permissions. Network state only reads whether a connection exists, and Lunavi has no internet permission.

05

Export & Sharing

You can export your data as JSON, CSV, ICS or PDF, and import a file you choose. Nothing is exported unless you ask. Export files are not encrypted, so anyone who has the file can read it. When you share or preview a file, it goes to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy applies. Lunavi has no cloud backup: Android backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for its data. To change phones, export a JSON file and import it on the new phone. App settings and your PIN are not included.

06

Google Play

The app itself sends nothing to us. Google Play provides us with the following, and Google's privacy policy applies to it:

  • Crash reports: through Android vitals, Google collects crash data from users who opted in to share usage and diagnostics data. We see details such as device model, OS version and stack traces, and use them to fix bugs. Lunavi adds no health data or personal information to them.
  • Store statistics: Play Console shows us totals for installs, ratings and store visits, such as by country and device.
  • Ratings and reviews: Lunavi can show Google's in-app review prompt. Google handles any rating through your Google account, and we can see the ratings and reviews you post.
07

No Advertising

Lunavi contains zero advertisements. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. No advertising identifiers are collected or used.

No AdMob. No tracking SDKs. No advertising identifiers — not even anonymized ones.

08

Your Rights

EEA & UK (GDPR): Lunavi's offline design means the app transfers no personal data off your device, which supports GDPR data minimisation and storage limitation. You can view, export and delete your data in the app at any time. Apart from the Google Play data above, we hold only what you email us, and you can ask us to delete it.

California (CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information. The app does not transmit sensitive personal information, including health data.

09

Data Retention & Deletion

Lunavi keeps your data on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. You can:

  • Delete individual entries, cycles, or all data directly within the app
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → Lunavi → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data permanently

There is no remote account to delete, and we cannot retrieve deleted data because we never hold a copy. Export files you saved or shared elsewhere are not removed.

Two kinds of copy can stay on your device. Before a database upgrade, Lunavi keeps one encrypted safety copy as a safeguard, so entries you delete one by one may remain in it until you use Delete all data, clear app data or uninstall. Copies of files you export stay unencrypted in the app's private cache until Android clears them, you clear app data or you uninstall. Delete all data does not remove them.

10

Children

Lunavi is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has entered data into Lunavi, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

11

Contact

Lunavi is published by Abhi Labs, Bangalore, India. Questions or privacy requests? Email us at contact@abhilabs.app. If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. The app's Request Feature / Report Bug option opens your email app with your device maker, model, Android version and app version filled in. No health data is added, and you can edit the message before sending. We use emails only to reply, and will delete them if you ask.
