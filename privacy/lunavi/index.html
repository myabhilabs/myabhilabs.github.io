Privacy Policy

Lunavi · Last updated: June 2026

Summary

Lunavi operates entirely offline with zero cloud uploads. Your logged periods, symptoms, moods, and AI-generated ovulation predictions never leave your device. We do not own, operate, or rent servers for user data storage.

Data We Collect

We do not collect personal data. No account is required. Lunavi contains no advertising SDKs and displays zero advertisements. The following clarifies what stays on your device:

  • Cycle data, symptoms, moods, and predictions are stored in a local Android Room database
  • The developer cannot access this information — there is no server connection
  • No data is transmitted to any server we operate

Health & Cycle Data

All health and cycle data you enter — including period dates, symptoms, moods, notes, and AI-generated ovulation predictions — is stored exclusively in a localised, secure Android Room database on your device. This data is never transmitted to any server or shared with any third party. Because no remote account exists, the developer has no ability to access your health data.

Crash Reporting

Anonymized crash reports are collected via Google Play Vitals to help us fix bugs. These reports contain device model, OS version, and stack traces only. Health data, cycle data, and any personal information are explicitly excluded from crash reports.

No Advertising

Lunavi contains zero advertisements. We do not use third-party advertising SDKs. No advertising identifiers are collected or used.

Your Rights

EEA & UK (GDPR): Lunavi's offline-first design means no personal data is transferred outside your device, inherently satisfying GDPR data minimisation and storage limitation principles. You have the right to delete all your data at any time.

California (CPRA): We do not sell or share personal information. No sensitive personal information (including health data) is transmitted.

Data Deletion

You have absolute, immediate control over your data:

  • Delete individual entries, cycles, or all data directly within the app
  • Clear all app data via Android Settings → Apps → Lunavi → Storage → Clear Data
  • Uninstall the app to remove all data permanently

There is no remote account to delete. Local deletion is permanent and irrecoverable — we cannot retrieve deleted data.

Children

Lunavi is not directed at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has entered data into Lunavi, you can delete it by clearing app data or uninstalling the app.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests? Email us at contact@abhilabs.app.
