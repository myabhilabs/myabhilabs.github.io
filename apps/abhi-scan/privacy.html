Privacy Policy

Updated May 9, 2026 Encryption Active

1. Overview

At Abhi Labs, we apply MNC-grade engineering to ensure your data never leaves your device. "AbhiScan: Offline PDF Scanner" is built with a "Privacy-by-Default" architecture. Your scans are for your eyes only.

2. Data Sovereignty

We utilize hardware-accelerated on-device processing. Every scan, OCR result, and PDF document remains strictly within your device's sandboxed environment. We collect zero scan data. No cloud uploads, no account required.

Zero_Cloud_Uploads
Local_Only_Storage

3. Device Permissions

Camera Access

Used exclusively for capturing documents. Image data is processed in real-time for edge detection and is never transmitted off-device.

Gallery & Storage

Used to import existing images for PDF conversion or to save your generated PDF documents to your device's storage.

4. Offline OCR Policy

Text extraction (OCR) is performed entirely on your device using local neural engines. Extracted text is indexed using a local SQLite FTS4 engine for full-text search. No text content is ever sent to external OCR servers.

  • Local Text Extraction
  • On-Device Indexing
  • Private Search
  • hardware Acceleration
