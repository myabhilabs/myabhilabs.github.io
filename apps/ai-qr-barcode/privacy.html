Privacy Policy

Updated: Feb 18, 2026 Verified Secure

1. Overview

At Abhi Labs, we believe privacy is a fundamental right. The "AI QR Barcode Scanner: Create" application is designed with a "Privacy-by-Default" architecture. This app is ad-supported and provided at no cost.

2. Local-First Storage

We utilize a secure local Room Database. This means every piece of data you generate or scan remains strictly within your device's sandboxed environment.

No Cloud Uploads
No Tracking

3. Device Permissions

Camera Access

Used exclusively for real-time 1D/2D code detection using Google ML Kit. We do not record or save video frames.

Storage & Gallery

Only accessed when you choose to import an image for scanning or export a generated QR code to your gallery.

4. Smart Actions Policy

Our AI engine analyzes the Intent of scanned data to provide smart actions (e.g., connecting to WiFi, sending SMS, or UPI verification).

  • Local WiFi Auth
  • Sandboxed Browsing
  • Direct SMS Triggers
  • UPI Intent Verification
